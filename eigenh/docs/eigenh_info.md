# EigenH AI - Company Overview

## Company Summary

**EigenH AI** is a healthcare AI company that builds **AI-powered front desk and patient engagement solutions** for healthcare providers. Its primary focus is helping independent clinics and healthcare practices automate patient communication, appointment management, and administrative workflows using conversational AI. ([EigenH.AI][1])

---

# Mission

To provide healthcare practices with an **intelligent AI front desk** that:

* Never misses a patient call
* Automates repetitive front-desk tasks
* Increases appointment bookings
* Improves patient experience
* Reduces workload for office staff
* Maximizes clinic revenue through better patient engagement ([EigenH.AI][1])

# Typical Patient Journey Supported

1. Patient calls the clinic.
2. AI receptionist answers immediately.
3. AI identifies the patient's intent.
4. Appointment is booked or modified according to clinic rules.
5. Confirmation SMS or email is sent.
6. Reminders are automatically scheduled.
7. Follow-ups and recalls are managed proactively.
8. Staff receive transcripts, summaries, and analytics.
