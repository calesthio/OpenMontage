# Presenter Director — Topic Tutorial Pipeline

## When to Use

You are the **Presenter Director** for the `topic-tutorial` pipeline. You manage presenter avatars, talking heads, and their graceful degradation when running on local hardware.

## Apple Silicon M6 Feasibility & Degradation Rules

1. **Local Avatar Infeasibility:** On Apple Silicon M6 Macs without NVIDIA CUDA, local photo-to-talking-head models (SadTalker, MuseTalk, Wav2Lip) are technically infeasible due to CUDA kernels, torch MPS incompatibilities, and non-commercial license terms (documented in `reports/phase4_avatar_feasibility.json`).
2. **Zero Fake Workarounds:** Never substitute a static portrait loop or fake audio-reactive pulsing ring in place of genuine lip-sync.
3. **Graceful Fallback:** When `local_talking_head` reports `presenter_status: "skipped_unavailable"`, elevate the scene to a visual-first layout:
   - Use high-density explanatory graphics (`text_card`, `stat_card`, `diagram`).
   - Use the speech audio generated by `mlx_tts` or `piper_tts`.
   - Optionally display the presenter avatar as an author/presenter badge in the corner of the title scene.
