# 5. Reviews, referrals & offline marketing — the compounding channels

Everything in this file is free, and every item here makes the *next* booking easier.

---

## A. The review engine

Google reviews are the single biggest factor in whether your profile ranks and whether a
caller trusts you. **You do not need to buy them** (buying them loses the profile). You need
to *ask*, consistently. Most happy customers simply never get asked.

**Where to get your review link:** Google Business Profile → **Ask for reviews** → copy the
short link (`https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXX/review`). Save it as WhatsApp quick reply `/review`.

### When to ask

| Timing | Why |
|---|---|
| Immediately after drop-off, while they're still in the car | Highest response rate — ask them to do it right there |
| Same evening on WhatsApp | Second-best; they're home and relaxed |
| 3 days after the trip | Follow-up for people who ignored the first ask |

### Scripts

**1. In the car, on arrival (best conversion — say it out loud):**
> "Sir/Madam, if you were happy with the trip, please give us a Google review — it helps a
> small business like ours a lot. I'll send you the link on WhatsApp right now."

**2. WhatsApp, same evening:**
```
🙏 Namaskar [Name] ji, A1 Tour and Travels madhe travel kelyabaddal dhanyavaad.
Tumcha anubhav kasa hota? A 30-second Google review our small business la khup madat karto:
<your review link>
Kahi problem asel tar direct mala call kara: 7888007234. — A1 Tour and Travels
```

**3. Follow-up (3 days later, only once):**
```
Namaskar [Name] ji 🙏 Just a gentle reminder — tumhi santusht asal tar hyalink click karun
1 minute madhe review deu shakta ka? <link> Dhanyavaad!
```

**4. Handwritten card** for premium/corporate travellers:
> "Thank you for travelling with A1 Tour and Travels. If we served you well, a Google review
> means a lot to our family business. — Scan / visit: <link>"

### What NOT to do

- ❌ Never write reviews yourself or ask staff/family to post fake ones.
- ❌ Never offer money for a review (a discount for *any* honest review is also against Google's
  policy — the referral offer in section C is for *bookings*, which is allowed).
- ❌ Don't ask only the happy ones in a way that filters out problems — Google detects review-gating.

### Reply to every review (this doubles the ranking benefit)

**5★ reply:**
> Thank you so much, [Name] ji! 🙏 It was a pleasure driving you. We'll be ready whenever you
> travel next — 7888007234. — Team A1 Tour and Travels

**3★ reply:**
> Thank you for the honest feedback, [Name] ji. Sorry the trip wasn't perfect — we've noted
> your point about [issue] and are fixing it. Please give us another chance: 7888007234.

**1★ reply (stay calm, never argue):**
> Namaskar [Name] ji, we're sorry about your experience. This isn't the standard we want.
> We'd like to understand what happened and make it right — please call me directly on
> 7888007234. — Owner, A1 Tour and Travels

A calm, professional reply to a bad review often converts readers *better* than a wall of 5★.

## B. Where else reviews matter (free, 2 minutes each)

| Platform | How to ask |
|---|---|
| **Facebook Page** | Enable **Recommendations**, then ask at the same time as the Google review |
| **Justdial / Sulekha / IndiaMART** | Send the listing link to repeat customers; reviews show publicly |
| **TripAdvisor** | For tour-package customers |
| **Your own website** | Ask permission, use their real words + first name + city (replace the sample testimonials) |

**Target:** 4 Google reviews/week for 8 weeks = 32 reviews. At 25+ reviews with regular
recency you will outrank most local operators who have none.

## C. Referral scheme (₹0 cost until a booking happens)

**Offer:** *Refer anyone; when they complete a trip, you get ₹200 off your next booking.*

Give ₹200 off — not cash. You only pay it out of a real booking, so the cost is zero until
someone uses you. It also pulls the referrer back for another trip, which is the real prize.

**Ask at the moment of highest goodwill** — right after drop-off, in the same message as the
review request:

```
🙏 Dhanyavaad [Name] ji!
Friend/relative la cab havi asel tar amcha number share kara — refer kelyavar
tumhala pudchya booking var ₹200 discount milel. 📞 7888007234
```

**Your top 20 repeat customers are worth more than any ad.** Keep a list of them and send them
a "Priority Customer" message before every long weekend:

```
🙏 Namaskar [Name] ji — aaplya priority customer list madhun message karat ahe.
Long weekend la bookings bharat ahet; tumhala pahije asel tar aadhi kalva.
📞 7888007234 — A1 Tour and Travels
```

## D. Offline marketing (very effective in this business, near-zero cost)

**1. Vehicle branding** — your best advertisement is the car itself. Get a simple vinyl sticker
on both rear side windows and the rear bumper:

```
A1 TOUR AND TRAVELS
PUNE ⇄ MUMBAI  |  MATCH CAB  |  AC BUS
📞 7888007234  |  💬 WhatsApp
```

Cost: a few hundred rupees for a set, once. Every jam on the expressway is a captive audience.

**2. Driver visiting card + passenger leaflet** — hand one to every passenger at drop-off.
Two-sided card: front = name/phone/WhatsApp QR; back = the fare chart (sedan ₹2500–3000,
SUV ₹3500–4500, Tempo ₹6000–8000, bus seat ₹500–800).

**3. WhatsApp QR poster** — print A5 posters with a big QR code that opens
`https://wa.me/917888007234?text=Hi%2C%20I%20want%20to%20book%20a%20Pune-Mumbai%20cab`
and the line *"Scan → Send → Get your fare in 5 minutes"*. Ask to put them up at:
- Petrol pumps on the Pune–Mumbai route and near Hinjewadi/Kharadi IT parks
- Tea stalls and dhabas on the old Mumbai–Pune highway
- Small hotels/lodges in Lonavala, Khandala, Talegaon
- Your regular customers' shops, puncture/tyre shops, garages
- Local doctor clinics, diagnostic labs (they refer patients travelling to Mumbai for treatment)

**4. Local sponsorship & groups** — free or ₹0–500: sponsor a local cricket/gully team, put a
banner at a Ganpati mandal or Navratri event, or offer a free airport run as a prize in a
housing-society event. Housing society WhatsApp groups in Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner, Kharadi,
Pimpri are goldmines for airport-run bookings — ask a resident friend to introduce you.

**5. Airport/station drivers' network** — the auto and taxi drivers waiting at Pune airport
often get requests they can't serve (outstation, groups). Leave cards with them; offer ₹300
for a converted referral. Costs nothing until it works.

## E. A 20-minute weekly routine that compounds

- **Every trip:** ask in the car → send WhatsApp review link in the evening.
- **Every Friday:** send the long-weekend message to your top 20 repeat customers.
- **Every Saturday:** 1 Google post, 1 photo, reply to all reviews.
- **Every Sunday:** post the week's best customer review as a story/social post.
- **Every month:** count your sources (how many calls from Google vs referrals) and drop
  whatever isn't producing.
