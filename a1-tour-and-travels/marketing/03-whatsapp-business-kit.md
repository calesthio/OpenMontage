# 3. WhatsApp Business kit — where your bookings actually happen

In India a travel business runs on WhatsApp. This kit turns your number into a
booking machine that answers the same five questions for you, all day.

**Install:** *WhatsApp Business* (free, separate app from normal WhatsApp). You can keep your
existing number — WhatsApp Business supports transferring your chat history, and you keep the
same 7888007234 that is printed on the website.

---

## 1. Profile setup (10 minutes)

**Business name:** `A1 Tour and Travels`
**Category:** Travel & Transportation → *Taxi service*
**Address:** Pune, Maharashtra (service-area business — no street address needed)
**Hours:** always open (24 hours, every day)

**About / status line** (139 char limit):

```
24x7 Pune ↔ Mumbai Cabs, Match Cabs & AC Buses. All India Tour Packages. Call/WhatsApp 7888007234
```

**Profile photo:** your logo. **Cover photo:** your best vehicle photo (or the site hero image).

## 5. Status / Stories plan (free reach — your contacts see it every day)

Post **1 status per day**, rotate these eight, always with your number visible:

1. Vehicle photo + "Aaj Pune → Mumbai sedan available. 7888007234"
2. Interior/boot shot + "Clean, sanitised, AC. 4/7/17 seater available"
3. A customer review screenshot + their name blurred → "Another happy family 🙏"
4. Highway/expressway video from a live trip + "On the road now — next slot open"
5. Fare card image: the four prices, big text
6. Driver photo with licence/ID blurred + "Verified & experienced drivers"
7. Airport run at 4am photo + "24x7 means 24x7 ✈️"
8. Weekend group photo (tempo traveller full) + "Sog sathi tempo traveller available"

## 6. Customer service habits that generate repeat business

- Reply **within 5 minutes** during the day, 15 minutes at night. Speed is your only real
  advantage over the big aggregators.
- Send the driver's **name + number + vehicle number** 2 hours before pickup. This one
  message removes 90% of customer anxiety.
- After drop-off, send `/thanks`. Two days later, send `/review`.
- Save every passenger as a contact: `Rahul Pune-Mumbai 12Oct`. That is your broadcast list.
- Never argue about a fare in chat. Give the fixed price once, and honour it.
