# 1. Google Business Profile — the highest-value free marketing you have

When someone in Pune types *"cab to Mumbai"*, *"Pune Mumbai taxi"* or *"taxi near me"*, Google
shows a **map pack** of three businesses. That is where calls come from — not from page-one
website links. This profile is how you get in it. It is completely free.

**Time needed:** ~7 minutes + OTP on 7888007234.

---

## Step 1 — Create the profile

1. Go to **https://business.google.com** (on a phone is easiest, or desktop is fine).
2. Sign in with a Google account. Use an account you will keep forever — if you have a
   business Gmail like `a1tourandtravels@gmail.com`, use that. Otherwise create one first.
3. Business name → paste exactly:

```
A1 Tour and Travels
```

> Do **not** stuff keywords into the name ("A1 Tour and Travels Pune Mumbai Best Taxi").
> Google suspends profiles for that. The name you use here must match your social pages,
> listings and vehicle boards.

4. Category → **Taxi service** (primary).
   Add secondary categories: **Bus tour agency**, **Tour operator**, **Car rental agency**,
   **Airport shuttle service**.
5. Select **"No, I don't have a location customers can visit"** → *service area business*.
   This is correct for you: you go to the customer. It hides the address and shows your
   service areas instead — which is what you want.
6. Service areas to add (add all of them):

7. Phone number → `7888007234`. Website → your live link (see `README.md`, section 2).
8. **Verification:** Google will offer phone/SMS, email, or video verification.
   - If video verification is offered, take it: film your cars, the number plate, your
     driving licence, and the inside of a vehicle in one continuous shot while walking
     around. Profiles verified by video go live fastest.
   - If postcard verification is offered instead, order it — it takes 5–14 days.

**Opening hours:** mark **"Open 24 hours"** for all seven days.

**Attributes** to tick (whatever is offered):

- Payment: **Cash, UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe**
- **Women-led** / **Veteran-led** → tick only if true for you

## Step 4 — Photos (profiles with photos get far more clicks)

Upload at least **10** in the first sitting. Free sources: your own phone gallery.
Shot list that Google rewards:

1. Exterior of each vehicle, number plate clearly visible, 3/4 angle, daylight
2. Clean interior — seats, dashboard, AC vents
3. A driver in uniform standing beside the car (builds trust)
4. Boot open showing luggage space
5. A customer being dropped off (ask permission first)
6. A highway/expressway shot from your own trip

Name files meaningfully before upload: `pune-to-mumbai-sedan-cab.jpg`,
`tempo-traveller-17-seater-pune.jpg`. Add a logo as the profile avatar and a wide cover photo.

## Step 5 — Posts (1 per week keeps you active; 3 is better)

Google Posts appear in your profile and expire after ~6 months. Recycle from
`04-social-media-content-pack.md`. Every post should carry **Call now** or **WhatsApp** as the
button, pointing at `tel:7888007234` / `https://wa.me/917888007234`.

## Step 6 — Seed the Q&A

Anyone can ask questions on your profile, and anyone can answer — including you. Ask a friend
or a second Google account to post these, then answer them from the business account:

- *Do you provide Pune to Mumbai cab at night?* → "Yes, we run 24x7. Call 7888007234 any hour."
- *What is the fare for a Pune to Mumbai cab?* → "Sedan ₹2500–3000, SUV ₹3500–4500 one way, toll and parking included."
- *Do you pick up from Pune airport?* → "Yes, doorstep pickup and drop at both Pune and Mumbai airports."

## Step 7 — Reviews (this is what ranks you)

The profile ranks largely on review count, recency and replies. Use the scripts in
`05-reviews-referrals-offline.md`. Your short review link looks like
`https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXX/review` — find it in the profile under **"Ask for reviews"**.
Save it as a WhatsApp quick-reply named `/review` so you can send it in one tap.

**Target: 4 new reviews a week for the first 8 weeks.** Twenty real reviews beats any
"SEO service" someone will try to sell you.
