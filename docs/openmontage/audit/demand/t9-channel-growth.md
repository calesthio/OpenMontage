## 4. Verdict: LONG_FORM_VIABLE

**Sample size:** 15 channels (MEASURED).

**LONG_FORM_VIABLE.** 9 of 15 channels (60%) broke past 50k views on at least one video. All 9 published exclusively long-form content on a narrow topic with regular cadence. The 6 that didn't break out either mixed Shorts with long-form, uploaded extremely infrequently, or had very low overall views.

**Pattern strength:** MODERATE. n=15 is a moderate sample. The correlation between "all long-form + regular cadence + narrow topic" and breakout is strong (9/9 breakout channels fit this pattern; 0/6 non-breakout channels fit it fully), but causality cannot be confirmed from observational data.

**First 10 videos of breakout channels:** All long-form (8-33 min), narrow topic focus, consistent duration within each channel, initial views typically low (under 2k) except channels that broke out immediately.
