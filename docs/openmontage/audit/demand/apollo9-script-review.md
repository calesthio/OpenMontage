A9-037 states "An S-IVB restart after transposition and docking produced less than the desired maximum velocity increase, off nominal by about 11 percent; it did not affect Apollo 9 but a lunar mission might have been aborted." The script's "nearly derailed the entire Moon program" is a reasonable paraphrase of "a lunar mission might have been aborted." |
| 4 | "Here is what actually happened, from NASA's own records." | [claim:A9-001] | **MISSATTRIBUTION.** A9-001 states "Apollo 9 was an Earth-orbital engineering test and the first flight of the full Apollo spacecraft."
