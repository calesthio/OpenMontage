# Apollo 9 Fact Pack

**Date compiled:** 2026-10-19  
**Scope:** Apollo 9 (LM Spider / CSM Gumdrop Earth-orbital engineering test, March 3-13 1969).  
**Method:** Every claim paraphrased from retrieved NASA primary pages (URLs cited). No quotes over 15 words — all claims are paraphrased. No mission facts stated from memory.  
**Status key:** TWO_SOURCES = claim supported by two independent retrieved sources; SINGLE_SOURCE = supported by one retrieved source.  
**Rights:** NASA pages are U.S. government works (public domain); NASA logos/insignia restricted (14 CFR 1221).
