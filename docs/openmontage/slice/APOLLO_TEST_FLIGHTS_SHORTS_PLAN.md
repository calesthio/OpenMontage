"Most people think Apollo went straight to the Moon. It didn't. Before Apollo 11, NASA flew Apollo 7, 8, 9, and 10 — each one retiring a specific risk. Apollo 8 was the first crewed ship to leave Earth orbit and reach the Moon. Apollo 9 tested the Lunar Module in Earth orbit. Apollo 10 flew the lander to within 50,000 feet of the surface — then came home. All of it rode the Saturn V, 363 feet tall, 7.6 million pounds of thrust, thirteen launches, zero crew losses. Those test flights turned a Moon landing from an idea into a repeatable operation."
