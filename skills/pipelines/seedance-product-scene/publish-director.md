# Publish Director — Final Delivery

## Inputs

Required: `render_report`, `final_review`, and draft `product_delivery`.

## Final Inventory

For every selected style verify:

- three candidate images and one selected image;
- one approved Seedance source video;
- one final music-muxed video;
- three copy variants;
- music title, artist, and license;
- relative paths inside the project.

Do not regenerate paid assets in this stage.

## Approval

Present the final package and offer:

1. Approve delivery.
2. Revise copy only.
3. Return to music selection.
4. Stop and preserve the project for later.

On approval, write final `product_delivery` and `publish_log`, then point the
user to the images, videos, copy, and project directory. 用户未审批时停止。
