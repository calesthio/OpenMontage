# 6. SEO & partnership growth — how to own the Pune ↔ Mumbai search

You rank for two different things, and they need different work:

1. **The map pack** (Google Maps results) → driven by your Google Business Profile, reviews
   and local citations. Covered in `01` and `02`. **This is 80% of your calls.**
2. **The blue links** (organic web results) → driven by your website's pages, content and links.

Do #1 first. It is faster and bigger for a local transport business.

## D. Free link building (backlinks that matter locally)

Google treats a link from a local, relevant site as a vote. Free ones worth getting:

1. **All the directory listings in `02`** — each one is a backlink to your site.
2. **Local news & blogs** — Pune/Mumbai travel bloggers, "things to do in Lonavala" posts.
   Offer a free airport transfer as a competition prize, or a genuine travel tip quote.
3. **Pune subreddit / Quora** — answer real questions ("What's the cheapest way from Pune to
   Mumbai?"). Give a genuinely useful answer with prices; mention your number only at the end.
   This drives actual traffic — and Quora answers rank in Google for years.
4. **Your partners' websites** — hotels, lodges, resorts and tour operators you drive for.
   Ask for a "Travel desk" or "Recommended taxi partner" listing with a link.
5. **Housing society / corporate notice boards** — the digital version (society apps,
   office travel groups) links and referrals both.
6. **Google Business Profile posts** — not a backlink, but they rank and get seen.

❌ Avoid paid "1000 backlinks for ₹500" packages. They get your site de-indexed.

## E. Partnership outreach (fastest route to repeat corporate revenue)

Free to do, high value. Print 20 cards, then visit/call these in this order:

| Target | What to offer |
|---|---|
| **Hotels & lodges in Lonavala, Khandala, Talegaon** | You are their airport/city transfer partner. They keep a cut; you get steady weekend bookings. |
| **Pune & Mumbai hotels near railway stations** | Guest airport runs. Leave cards at the front desk. |
| **Wedding planners & photographers** | Guest transport, 2–3 vehicles per wedding. Big ticket, frequent. |
| **Corporate travel desks (Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Magarpatta IT parks)** | Fixed monthly rate for employee airport runs; single point of contact. |
| **Hospitals & diagnostic centres in Pune** | Patients travelling to Mumbai for treatment. Sensitive, reliable service = long relationship. |
| **Travel agents & tour operators** | You become their ground transport in Pune/Mumbai. |
| **Marriage halls, banquet halls** | Guest shuttle on event days. |
| **Schools & colleges** | Annual trips, picnics, industrial visits — Tempo Traveller and bus hires. |

**The pitch that works** (say this, short and specific):

> "Namaskar, I run A1 Tour and Travels — Pune to Mumbai cabs, tempo travellers and buses,
> 24x7. I'm not asking for money. I want to be the number you call when your guests need a
> ride: one call, fixed fare, confirmed vehicle. If you send me a booking, I'll take care of
> your guest like my own family. Can I leave 10 cards at your desk?"

**Follow up once, 10 days later.** Then once a month with a simple WhatsApp:
`"Namaskar, koi booking asel tar sanga — 7888007234."` Most partnerships need 3 touches.

## F. Month-by-month expectations (so you don't give up early)

| Month | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Profile verified, 10+ photos, 12–20 reviews. Calls start trickling from Maps. |
| 2 | Listings live → more "near me" impressions. 25+ reviews. Ranking improves noticeably. |
| 3 | You start appearing in the map pack for some Pune/Mumbai queries. Referrals compound. |
| 4–6 | Organic clicks grow as route pages get built. Corporate/partner repeat bookings become a base load. |

Don't chase ads until these are done. A free profile with 40 real reviews will beat a
₹10,000/month Google Ads budget spent against nothing.

## G. The one number to watch

**Calls per week, by source.** Nothing else matters early on. Write it down every Sunday.
If a channel produced nothing for 4 straight weeks, stop doing it and double whatever did.
