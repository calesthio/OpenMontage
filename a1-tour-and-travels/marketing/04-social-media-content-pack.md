## Instagram-specific notes

- **Bio:** `Pune ↔ Mumbai Cabs • Match Cabs • AC Buses • All India Tours | 24x7 | 📞 7888007234 | Book on WhatsApp 👇` + your site link
- **Highlights to create:** `Fares`, `Fleet`, `Reviews`, `Tours`, `Contact`
- **Reels beat photos** for reach. Shoot vertical, 9:16, 15–25 seconds, add captions
  (most people watch on mute), and always show the number on screen.
- Reply to every DM within the hour — Instagram shows your page to more people when you do.
- Post the **same photo to Facebook Marketplace** ("Service offered → Travel") — free local reach.

## Facebook-specific notes

- Turn on **Messenger** and set the same greeting message as your WhatsApp.
- Post in local **Pune groups** ("Pune Buy Sell", "Pune Travel", "Pune Flatmates") only where
  group rules allow service posts — lead with value (a fare chart), not a bare ad. One post
  per group per fortnight, or you'll be removed.
- Ask 5 friends to **recommend the page** — recommendation count shows publicly.
