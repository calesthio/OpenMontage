## 4. Cross-mission notes

- All three missions' primary sources are **NASA public domain** (U.S. government works), reusable without a key. Logos/insignia restricted (14 CFR 1221). Corrected/annotated journal content (Apollo Lunar Surface Journal) may carry editor copyright — use raw transcripts for reuse.
- NTRS mission reports (PDF) are the deepest technical source for burn-by-burn, staging, alarm, and procedure detail — exactly what recent long-form videos lack (see T14).
- The Apollo Flight Journal (all missions) and Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (Apollo 11+) provide annotated transcripts ideal for verifying "what they said" during critical phases.
