## Contradictions / open ranges
- C-1 Titan gravity: T7 (Scientific American) quotes "about a tenth as strong" as Earth, while T1/T2/T5 say ~1/7 of Earth. Minor discrepancy (~1/7 vs ~1/10); treat gravity as "well below Earth's, roughly 1/7."
- C-2 Titan size framing: T1 (NASA) notes Titan was long considered the largest moon until Ganymede took the record; T8 (Dragonfly FS) states Titan is larger than Mercury. Both are compatible (Titan > Mercury but < Ganymede) — note the different comparison baselines.
