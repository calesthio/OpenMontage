# 2. Free listings & directories — get found everywhere people look

Rule zero: **the name, phone number and service areas must be character-for-character
identical on every listing.** Google cross-checks them and rewards consistency. Copy-paste
from this file, never retype from memory.

**Canonical NAP block — use this everywhere:**

```
Business name : A1 Tour and Travels
Phone         : 7888007234  (international: +91 78880 07234)
WhatsApp      : https://wa.me/917888007234
Website       : <your live URL>
Service areas : Pune, Mumbai, Lonavala, Nashik, Shirdi, All India
Hours         : Open 24 hours, all days

                Tempo Traveller hire, airport transfers, All India tour packages
```

## A. Where to list — in priority order

| # | Platform | Cost | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Google Business Profile** | Free | Calls + Maps | see `01-google-business-profile.md` |
| 2 | **Justdial** | Free listing | Huge in Pune/Mumbai for local services | justdial.com → "List your business" → free plan. Ignore the paid upsell. |
| 3 | **Sulekha** | Free listing | Strong for cab/travel | sulekha.com → partner signup → free package |
| 4 | **IndiaMART** | Free | Buyers search "tempo traveller hire" | indiamart.com → sell → free seller account |
| 5 | **Bing Places** | Free | Powers Bing + some Windows/Copilot answers | bingplaces.com — you can **import from Google** in 2 clicks |
| 6 | **Apple Business Connect** | Free | Apple Maps + Siri | businessconnect.apple.com |
| 7 | **Facebook Page** | Free | Credibility, reviews, Marketplace reach | see `04-social-media-content-pack.md` |
| 8 | **Instagram Business** | Free | Visual proof of fleet | link it to the Facebook page |
| 9 | **OLX / Quikr** | Free | Classifieds still convert for outstation travel | Post under "Travel → Car/Bus rental", refresh weekly |
| 10 | **Yandex Business** | Free | Minor, 10 minutes | yandex.com/business |
| 11 | **TripAdvisor** | Free | Tour-package buyers check it | tripadvisor.in → "Add a business" |
| 12 | **Facebook Marketplace** | Free | Local reach, zero effort | "Service offered → Travel" |
| 13 | **WhatsApp Business catalogue** | Free | Converts enquiries instantly | see `03-whatsapp-business-kit.md` |
| 14 | **Pune/Mumbai local directories** | Free | Yellow Pages India, Trade India, Exportersindia, Cybo, Tupalo, Yelu | 5 minutes each, same NAP |

**Skip anything that asks for a card, an "SEO fee", or "guaranteed page 1".** Those are
cold-call lead resellers, not marketing.

## G. Categories & keywords to select on each platform

Primary: **Taxi service / Cab service / Car rental**
Also tick where offered: **Bus rental**, **Tour operator**, **Travel agency**,
**Airport transfer service**, **Tempo traveller on hire**, **Outstation taxi**.

## H. Weekly 15-minute maintenance routine

Do this every Saturday — it is what keeps free listings ranking:

1. Google Business Profile → add 1 photo, 1 post (copy from `04`).
2. Justdial / Sulekha → reply to any enquiry, refresh the listing if it allows.
3. OLX → bump/renew your two ads.
4. Ask the week's completed trips for a review (`05-reviews-referrals-offline.md`).
5. Check the phone number is still correct on every listing (copy-paste errors happen).
