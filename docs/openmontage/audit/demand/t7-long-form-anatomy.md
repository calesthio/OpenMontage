## 3. Hook style in descriptions (MEASURED)

All 24 videos follow a consistent hook pattern:

1. **Title repeated as first line** — The video title appears verbatim at the start of the description.
2. **1–2 sentence context hook** — A brief setup sentence(s) explaining what the video covers.
3. **External links** — Patreon, website, social media, or source links.
4. **Copyright/disclaimer text** — Common on older uploads (2012–2016).

Examples:
- "99 Unbelievable Aviation Moments Caught on Camera" → "From jaw-dropping near-misses to mind-blowing landings in extreme weather, this video captures the..."
- "The End Of The Apollo Program" → "Between the years 1968 and 1972, NASA sent 24 men to the moon. The final mission, Apollo 17, flew in December 1972..."
- "Apollo 13 The Real Story" → "For other infos, videos, transcripts and downloads visit our site: http://www.rmastri.it/spacestuff/missions/apollo-13"
